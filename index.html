01The problem

Three walls. Everyone hits the same ones.

Nothing about this business is hard. It's just undocumented — so most people stall in exactly the same three places.

  1. i

    You can't find a real supplier

    Search results are resellers with 500-unit minimums, or chips that won't hold a link. You lose two weeks and $60 on samples that don't work.

  2. ii

    You have no idea what to charge

    Price too low and it reads as a gimmick. Price blind and you can't answer “why that much?” — which is the moment the sale dies.

  3. iii

    You freeze at the door

    Without a script the pitch turns into an apology. The owner is busy, you're vague, and you walk out with a card still in your pocket.
